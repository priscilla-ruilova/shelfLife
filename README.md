# Shelf Life

### Intro:
Whether you live with roommates or you life with family, there's a million reasons why food goes bad in the fridge. 
- Maybe you thought it was your roommate's takeout container
- Maybe your roommate went on a trip and forgot to share what belonged to them
- Maybe the week was just super busy and you forgot to cook the patties you were defrosting...

This app is designed to prevent food waste and provide accountability for households by tracking fridge items.

Food waste?

Not in this house!

### Features:
- Start off with a log in or sign up page so you can curate your virtual fridge.
- Use the 'Add a fridge item' form to share some details about the food you plan to put away.
- Read what yourself and household members have stored away. 
- Make edits to firdge items by hitting the hyperlinked 'edit' word.
- Delete friddge items when you finish consuming them!
(I know I know, you might delete because the food went bad...)
